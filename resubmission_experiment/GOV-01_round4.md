# GOV-01: Ownership and Accountability (v4 — Round 4 Revision)
## StudySpark AI — Ashcombe University Learning Support Office

---

### 1. Named Accountable Owner

The named accountable owner is the **Head of Learning Support**, currently Dr. Fiona Marsh, who is personally responsible for StudySpark AI's deployment, content quality, and any decision to expand or withdraw the tool. Fiona approves any change to the system prompt, reviews monthly usage summaries, and is the point of escalation if a staff member has a concern about a drafted message.

---

### 2. Deputy and Successor Arrangement

The **Deputy Head of Learning Support**, currently Mr. Daniel Osei, is the designated deputy accountable owner, holding the same escalation authority and system prompt approval rights as Dr. Marsh during any period of her unavailability exceeding 3 working days. If both roles are vacant simultaneously, accountability transfers to the **Director of Student Services**. This chain is documented in the LSO Digital Tools Register, Section 3.

### 6. Content Quality Assurance Between Reviews

Content quality is assured continuously through three mechanisms operating between Dr. Marsh's monthly 10% sample review: a weekly peer spot-check rota, a self-certification checkbox at the point of sending, and escalation-triggered ad hoc review (see GOV-06 Incident Response).

---

### 7. Escalation Path for Complaints Bypassing the LSO *(new)*

In addition to the standard LSO-mediated escalation path (Section 2), StudySpark AI's accountability chain explicitly covers the scenario where a student complaint does **not** come through the LSO first — for example, a complaint raised directly with the **University Ombudsman** or the central **Student Complaints Office**.

**Process:** Under the university's standard Student Complaints Procedure, any complaint referencing an LSO communication is automatically routed by the Ombudsman/Complaints Office to the Head of Learning Support (or the Deputy, per Section 2's continuity arrangement) within 2 working days of receipt, as the named accountable owner for LSO digital tools per RP-LSO-001. Dr. Marsh is required to respond to the Ombudsman's office within 5 working days confirming whether StudySpark AI was involved in drafting the communication in question, and if so, providing the relevant session log reference (see TRAN-05) to support the Ombudsman's investigation.

This ensures that even a complaint that entirely bypasses direct contact with LSO staff still reaches the correct accountable owner promptly, without relying on the student or the Ombudsman knowing in advance who is responsible for the tool.
