# GOV-01: Ownership and Accountability
## StudySpark AI — Ashcombe University Learning Support Office

---

### Designated Owner

**Dr. Fiona Marsh, Head of Learning Support**, is the designated owner of StudySpark AI. She is personally accountable for the tool's deployment, for the quality of the guidance drafts it produces, and for any decision to change, expand, or withdraw it. This ownership is formally documented in the LSO Digital Tools Register, entry LSO-DTR-2026-014, dated 28 May 2026.

---

### Accountability When AI-Generated Guidance Causes Harm or Distress

If an AI-generated study guidance message causes harm or distress to a student, accountability rests with **Dr. Marsh as the tool owner and the individual LSO staff member who reviewed and sent the specific message**, since no message reaches a student without a named staff member personally reading, editing, and sending it — the AI drafts, but a human is always the final author of record. This dual accountability is deliberate: it ensures no harmful message can be attributed to "the AI" alone with no accountable person behind it.

If harm or distress is identified, Dr. Marsh personally reviews the specific message and its context, contacts the affected student directly to address the concern, and determines whether the incident reflects an isolated error or a pattern requiring a change to the tool's system prompt or its continued use.

---

### Escalation Pathway for Problematic Outputs

If any LSO staff member identifies a problematic AI-generated draft — before or after it is sent — the pathway is:

1. The staff member reports the issue to **Dr. Marsh directly**, by email or in person, as soon as it is identified.
2. Dr. Marsh reviews the specific draft or message within 2 working days.
3. If a student was affected, Dr. Marsh or the relevant staff member contacts the student to address the issue.
4. If the problem indicates a systemic risk rather than a one-off error, Dr. Marsh can immediately suspend staff access to StudySpark AI by removing it from the university SSO portal, with no approval required from anyone else to do so.

This escalation pathway applies to every problematic output regardless of severity — there is a single, clear point of contact and a standing authority to pause the tool immediately.
