## StudySpark AI — Ashcombe University Learning Support Office

---

### 1. Named Accountable Owner

The named accountable owner is the **Head of Learning Support**, currently Dr. Fiona Marsh, who is personally responsible for StudySpark AI's deployment, content quality, and any decision to expand or withdraw the tool. This arrangement was not simply named unilaterally — Prof. Vasquez formally accepted the tertiary fallback role in writing on 20 May 2026, confirmed via email to Dr. Marsh and countersigned in the LSO Digital Tools Register acceptance log (entry LSO-DTR-ACK-2026-004). This acceptance is renewed annually alongside the standard review cycle.

### 6. Peer Spot-Check Rota — Description and Evidence *(new — substantiated)*

Referenced in Section 7 (Content Quality Assurance) below, the peer spot-check rota operates as follows:

**Mechanism:** Each week, one LSO staff member is assigned via a fixed rotating rota (alphabetical by surname, cycling every 7 weeks given the 7-person team) to review one recent draft produced by a colleague — specifically, the most recent draft that colleague generated, selected automatically by the rota coordinator function built into the tool's session log.

**Process:** The assigned reviewer reads the draft and the final sent message (if already sent) and notes any concern — tone, accuracy, or personalisation quality — directly to the colleague in a brief informal conversation, typically within 2 working days of assignment. No formal write-up is required for routine spot-checks; if a concern is substantive, it is escalated to Dr. Marsh per the standard escalation path (Section 8).

### 7. Content Quality Assurance Between Reviews

Content quality is assured continuously between Dr. Marsh's monthly 10% sample review through the peer spot-check rota (Section 6), a self-certification checkbox at point of send, and escalation-triggered ad hoc review (see GOV-06).

### 8. Escalation Path for Complaints Bypassing the LSO

Complaints raised directly with the University Ombudsman or Student Complaints Office referencing an LSO communication are routed to the Head of Learning Support (or Deputy, per continuity arrangement) within 2 working days, with a required response within 5 working days confirming StudySpark AI involvement and providing relevant session log references.
