# GOV-01: Ownership and Accountability (v2 — Revised)
## StudySpark AI — Ashcombe University Learning Support Office

---

### 1. Named Accountable Owner

The named accountable owner is the **Head of Learning Support**, currently Dr. Fiona Marsh, who is personally responsible for StudySpark AI's deployment, content quality, and any decision to expand or withdraw the tool. Fiona approves any change to the system prompt, reviews monthly usage summaries, and is the point of escalation if a staff member has a concern about a drafted message.

---

### 2. Deputy and Successor Arrangement *(new)*

To ensure accountability continuity if Dr. Marsh is unavailable (leave, absence, or role change), the **Deputy Head of Learning Support**, currently Mr. Daniel Osei, is the designated deputy accountable owner. Mr. Osei holds the same escalation authority and system prompt approval rights as Dr. Marsh during any period of her unavailability exceeding 3 working days.

If both the Head and Deputy Head roles become vacant simultaneously, accountability transfers automatically to the **Director of Student Services**, who holds ultimate escalation authority for all LSO digital tools pending a substantive appointment. This escalation chain is documented in the LSO Digital Tools Register, Section 3 ("Continuity of Accountability").

---

### 3. Communication of Accountability to Staff *(new)*

All seven LSO staff members are informed of the accountability structure — including the named owner, deputy, and escalation pathway — through two channels:

1. **Onboarding briefing:** The 30-minute StudySpark AI briefing (see HUMO-01) includes a dedicated slide naming Dr. Marsh as accountable owner, Mr. Osei as deputy, and explaining exactly how and when to escalate a concern (email, in-person, or via the LSO Digital Tools Register incident log).
2. **Posted reference card:** A printed one-page "Who to Contact" card is displayed at each LSO staff workstation, listing the accountable owner, deputy, and the Director of Student Services as the final escalation point, with direct contact details for each.

Staff sign an acknowledgement at onboarding confirming they know who is accountable and how to escalate a concern; these acknowledgements are filed in the LSO staff training register.

---

### 4. Review Cadence *(new)*

The accountability arrangement documented here — including the named owner, deputy, and escalation chain — is formally reviewed **annually every June**, and additionally whenever the Head of Learning Support role changes hands. The most recent review was conducted 15 June 2026 and is logged as entry LSO-DTR-2026-014 in the LSO Digital Tools Register. The next scheduled review is June 2027.
