# GOV-01: Ownership and Accountability (v3 — Round 3 Revision)
## StudySpark AI — Ashcombe University Learning Support Office

---

### 1. Named Accountable Owner

The named accountable owner is the **Head of Learning Support**, currently Dr. Fiona Marsh, who is personally responsible for StudySpark AI's deployment, content quality, and any decision to expand or withdraw the tool. Fiona approves any change to the system prompt, reviews monthly usage summaries, and is the point of escalation if a staff member has a concern about a drafted message.

---

### 2. Deputy and Successor Arrangement

The **Deputy Head of Learning Support**, currently Mr. Daniel Osei, is the designated deputy accountable owner, holding the same escalation authority and system prompt approval rights as Dr. Marsh during any period of her unavailability exceeding 3 working days. If both roles are vacant simultaneously, accountability transfers to the **Director of Student Services**. This chain is documented in the LSO Digital Tools Register, Section 3.

---

### 3. Internal Communication of Accountability to Staff

All seven LSO staff are informed of the accountability structure through the onboarding briefing (see HUMO-01) and a posted "Who to Contact" reference card at each workstation. Staff sign an acknowledgement confirming they know who is accountable and how to escalate a concern.

---

### 4. External Communication of Accountability *(new)*

Accountability for StudySpark AI is also made visible outside the LSO team:

- **To students:** The LSO Privacy Notice (referenced in PRIV-02 and PRIV-03) names Dr. Fiona Marsh, Head of Learning Support, as the accountable contact for any question or concern about AI-assisted guidance drafting, with her university contact details provided.
- **To university stakeholders:** StudySpark AI is listed in the university's central **Digital Tools Register**, a register accessible to relevant university committees (Digital Governance Office, Student Services Board), which records Dr. Marsh as accountable owner, Mr. Osei as deputy, and links to this governance document. Any university stakeholder — for example, a department head whose students use the LSO — can identify the accountable owner via this central register without needing to contact the LSO directly.

---

### 5. Content Quality Assurance Between Reviews *(new)*

Dr. Marsh's monthly 10% sample review (see Human Oversight Plan) is the periodic assurance check, but content quality is also actively assured on an ongoing basis through three mechanisms operating *between* monthly reviews:

1. **Peer spot-check:** Staff are encouraged, and one is randomly assigned each week via a rota, to informally review one colleague's recent draft and note any concern directly to that colleague — a lightweight peer-assurance layer that does not wait for the monthly cycle.
2. **Self-certification at send:** Before sending, staff confirm via a single checkbox in the interface that they have personalised the draft and verified it against the student's actual circumstances — this is a self-assurance step logged at the point of use (see TRAN-05 for logging detail).
3. **Escalation-triggered review:** Any staff-reported concern (see GOV-06 Incident Response) triggers an immediate ad hoc content quality review by Dr. Marsh, rather than waiting for the next scheduled monthly sample.

Together, these three mechanisms mean content quality is monitored continuously, not solely at the monthly checkpoint.

---

### 6. Formal Role Profile *(new)*

The accountable owner's duties are codified independently of this document in the **LSO Head of Learning Support — Role Profile and Terms of Reference (RP-LSO-001)**, maintained by university HR and the Digital Governance Office. RP-LSO-001 includes a dedicated section, "Digital Tool Accountability," which formally assigns responsibility for all LSO digital tools — including StudySpark AI — to the postholder, independent of who currently holds the role. This ensures accountability is tied to the role itself, not solely to Dr. Marsh as an individual, and survives any future personnel change without requiring this governance document to be rewritten.

---

### 7. Review Cadence

This accountability arrangement is reviewed annually every June, and whenever the Head of Learning Support role changes hands. Last reviewed 15 June 2026, logged as LSO-DTR-2026-014. Next review: June 2027.
