# PRIV-02: Lawful Basis and Consent
## MediScan AI — GDPR Lawful Basis Assessment (LBA-MEDISCAN-2026-001)

**Lawful basis:** UK GDPR Article 9(2)(h) — processing necessary for medical diagnosis and the provision of health care treatment.

**Applies to:**
- Adult patient DICOM imaging data processed for diagnostic purposes.
- Biometric face scan data used for patient identity verification to prevent wrong-patient errors.
- Electronic health record data retrieved via HL7 FHIR for diagnostic context.

**Children (under 18):** Same Article 9(2)(h) basis applies. Supplemented by parental or guardian consent obtained during hospital admission.

**Data Processing Agreements in place:**
- AWS (model inference infrastructure) — DPA signed March 2026.
- Sri Lanka hospital network (joint controller) — DPA signed April 2026.

**DPO sign-off:** Confirmed as adequate by the Data Protection Officer, May 2026.
