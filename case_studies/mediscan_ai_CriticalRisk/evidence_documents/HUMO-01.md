# HUMO-01: Oversight Roles and Training
## MediScan AI — Clinical Oversight Framework (CAGF-2026-001)

**Defined oversight roles:**

| Role | Responsibility | Qualification Required |
|------|---------------|----------------------|
| Consultant Radiologist | Review and sign off all AI reports | GMC registration + 4hr MediScan AI training |
| Clinical Governance Lead | Weekly log review, incident management | Clinical governance qualification |
| CMO | Overall clinical accountability | Executive sign-off authority |
| Head of AI Engineering | Model performance and security | Technical accountability |

**Training requirement:** All clinical staff must complete the MediScan AI training programme before system access is granted. Training records held in HR system and reviewed annually.

**Access gate:** System access is blocked until training completion is recorded in the HR system.
