# PRIV-07: Cross-Border Data Transfer Assessment
## MediScan AI — Transfer Impact Assessments

**Three cross-border data flows identified:**

### Flow 1: UK → Sri Lanka Hospital Network
- **Purpose:** Multi-site clinical review of patient imaging data.
- **Mechanism:** Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) in inter-hospital data sharing agreement.
- **TIA reference:** TIA-MEDISCAN-LK-2026-001 — completed April 2026.

### Flow 2: UK/Sri Lanka → AWS US-East
- **Purpose:** LLaMA Vision model inference.
- **Mechanism:** AWS Data Processing Addendum incorporating SCCs. AWS HIPAA BAA in place.
- **TIA reference:** TIA-MEDISCAN-AWS-2026-001 — completed April 2026.

### Flow 3: Biometric face scan data
- Processed locally at hospital only. Does not leave the hospital network. No transfer occurs.

**MHRA notification:** Cross-border processing notified to MHRA as part of Class IIa medical device registration, May 2026.

**Review cycle:** All TIAs reviewed annually and following any change to data flows.

**Document Owner:** Data Protection Officer | June 2026
