# TRAN-02: System Limitations Stated
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

The following limitations are displayed on the tablet screen below the input field at all times:

- "Suggestions may not account for allergies — always check before cooking."
- "I don't know what equipment you have — adapt suggestions as needed."
- "Suggestions are ideas only. You decide what to cook."

**Additional limitations communicated verbally at introduction:**
- The tool does not know how many people will be eating or their individual dietary needs.
- It may occasionally suggest recipes requiring ingredients or techniques not available in a community kitchen setting.
- It cannot guarantee nutritional balance.
- It may repeat suggestions if similar ingredient lists are entered.

Volunteers understand that RecipeSuggest is a quick brainstorming aid, not a culinary authority. They are experienced enough to assess whether a suggestion is practical.
