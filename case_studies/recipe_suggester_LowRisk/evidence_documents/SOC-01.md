# SOC-01: Intended Societal Benefit
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

**Intended benefit:** RecipeSuggest helps Nourish Community Kitchen make better use of donated and surplus ingredients by giving volunteers quick ideas for what to cook. This reduces food waste and helps the kitchen serve more varied, nutritious meals to local residents who rely on the community kitchen for their main meal of the day.

**Who benefits:**
- Kitchen volunteers — spend less time deciding what to cook and more time cooking
- Kitchen visitors — receive a more varied meal rather than the same rotation of familiar recipes

**How benefit is recognised:** Sam Okafor notes informally whether volunteers report using suggestions and whether the variety of meals served has increased. No formal measurement is required at this scale.

**Societal risk:** Minimal. If the tool were removed tomorrow, volunteers would cook as they did before. The tool adds convenience; it does not create dependency or displace any human skill.

**Environmental consideration:** The tool encourages use of available ingredients rather than buying new ones, which modestly supports the kitchen's food waste reduction goals.

**Owner:** Sam Okafor, Volunteer Coordinator | June 2026
