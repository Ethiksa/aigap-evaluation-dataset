# RISK-02: Risk Classification
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

**Classification: Minimal risk.**

**Rationale:**
- No personal data is collected, processed, or stored
- No decision affecting any person's rights, health, finances, or opportunities is made or supported
- Users are experienced kitchen volunteers who exercise full independent judgment
- The worst plausible outcome is a suboptimal recipe suggestion, which the volunteer can simply ignore
- The system is used by 2 people in a closed internal environment
- No public-facing deployment
- No automated actions — the system only produces text suggestions

This classification places RecipeSuggest firmly within the AIGAP UG (Undergraduate) tier with a raw risk score of 18 out of 100. No mandatory overrides apply. The system does not approach the threshold for any higher risk classification under the EU AI Act or AIGAP's assurance framework.
