# GOV-02: Documented Purpose and Scope
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

**Intended purpose:** RecipeSuggest helps kitchen volunteers think of recipes when they are unsure what to cook with the ingredients available that day. The volunteer types a list of ingredients and the tool suggests up to three recipes.

**Intended users:** The two kitchen volunteers at Nourish Community Kitchen only.

**Operating context:** One tablet in the kitchen, used only during meal preparation sessions. No internet access except for the OpenAI API call.

**Explicit out-of-scope uses:**
- Suggesting recipes involving allergens without the volunteer checking first
- Making any food safety decision on behalf of the kitchen
- Use by anyone outside the kitchen volunteer team
- Use for any purpose other than meal planning during preparation sessions
- Storing or sharing any information about kitchen visitors or recipients of meals

The volunteer always decides what to cook. RecipeSuggest is a thinking aid, not a decision-maker.

**Owner:** Sam Okafor, Volunteer Coordinator | June 2026
