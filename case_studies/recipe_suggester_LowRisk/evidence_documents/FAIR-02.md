# FAIR-02: Bias Testing Plan
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

**Context:** RecipeSuggest does not make decisions affecting any person's rights, opportunities, or access to services. It suggests recipes from a list of ingredients. Formal bias testing methodologies designed for decision-making systems are not proportionate to this use case.

**Proportionate approach:** The two volunteers informally assess whether the suggestions feel culturally balanced — for example, whether the tool only suggests Western European recipes regardless of the ingredients entered. If suggestions appear systematically narrow, Sam Okafor will update the system prompt to encourage a broader range of cuisines.

**Threshold:** If either volunteer notices that suggestions are repeatedly inappropriate, unhelpful, or culturally one-sided over a two-week period, Sam will review and adjust the tool's instructions.

**Formal bias testing:** Not applicable at UG level for a zero-impact informational tool. This position will be revisited if the tool's scope ever expands beyond ingredient-to-recipe suggestions.

**Owner:** Sam Okafor, Volunteer Coordinator | June 2026
