# FAIR-07: User Recourse and Appeals
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

**Who could be harmed:** Only the two kitchen volunteers use the tool. No kitchen visitor or meal recipient interacts with it or is affected by its output in a way that could constitute a rights-based harm.

**Recourse for volunteers:** If a volunteer finds a suggestion unhelpful, inappropriate, or potentially unsafe, they simply do not use it. The volunteer has full authority to ignore any suggestion from RecipeSuggest at any time. No mechanism compels them to follow the tool's output.

**Reporting:** If a volunteer has a concern about the tool's behaviour, they raise it with Sam Okafor directly. Sam will review and either update the tool's instructions or remove it if the concern cannot be resolved.

**No formal appeals process is required** because the tool makes no decision affecting any person's rights, access to services, or opportunities. The recourse route is simply: ignore the suggestion and speak to Sam.

**Owner:** Sam Okafor, Volunteer Coordinator | June 2026
