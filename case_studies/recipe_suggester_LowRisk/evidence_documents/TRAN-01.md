# TRAN-01: User-Facing AI Disclosure
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

Users are informed that RecipeSuggest is an AI tool through three means:

1. **App label:** The tablet displays the name "RecipeSuggest — AI Recipe Tool" at the top of the screen at all times.

2. **Opening message:** When the app is opened, the screen shows: "I'm an AI tool. Type your available ingredients and I'll suggest some recipes. I'm not a food safety adviser — always use your own judgment."

3. **Volunteer briefing:** Both volunteers were told by Sam Okafor at introduction that the suggestions come from OpenAI's GPT-4o model, not from a human.

There is no possibility a volunteer could mistake the tool for a human, as the interface is a simple text input on a tablet with no conversational framing that could suggest otherwise.

**Owner:** Sam Okafor, Volunteer Coordinator | June 2026
