# GOV-01: Ownership and Accountability
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

**Named accountable owner:** Volunteer coordinator Sam Okafor is personally responsible for RecipeSuggest. This includes deciding whether to continue using the tool, updating the instructions given to it, and removing it if it causes problems.

**Responsibilities:**
- Approving any change to what the tool is asked to do
- Deciding whether suggestions from the tool are safe and appropriate to follow
- Removing the tool from the kitchen if it behaves unexpectedly

**How accountability is maintained:** The tool runs on a single tablet that Sam manages. No other person can change its configuration. If Sam leaves the role, the incoming coordinator takes over responsibility before the handover is complete.

No decisions made using RecipeSuggest affect any person's rights or safety. The tool suggests recipes; the volunteer decides whether to use them.

**Owner:** Sam Okafor, Volunteer Coordinator | June 2026
