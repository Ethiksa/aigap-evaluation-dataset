# TRAN-03: Explainability Appropriate to Context
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

**Explanation provided:** RecipeSuggest is instructed via its system prompt to briefly state why it is suggesting each recipe. A typical response looks like: "Lentil soup — uses all your main ingredients and is quick to prepare in large batches."

This one-line rationale is sufficient for the context because:
- The volunteer is choosing between recipe ideas, not making a high-stakes decision
- The connection between ingredients and recipe is self-evident to an experienced cook
- A detailed statistical or algorithmic explanation would add no practical value

**Why this level of explanation is appropriate:** RecipeSuggest operates in a domain where the user is an expert (an experienced kitchen volunteer) making a low-stakes choice (what to cook today). The AIGAP UG level does not require formal explainability mechanisms — a plain-language rationale per suggestion is proportionate and sufficient.

No individual's rights depend on the explanation, and no regulatory requirement for formal explanation applies to this use case.
