# FAIR-01: Protected Groups Considered
## RecipeSuggest — Nourish Community Kitchen

**Groups considered:**

**Kitchen visitors with dietary requirements or allergies:** RecipeSuggest does not and must not make decisions about what any individual visitor eats. The volunteer retains full responsibility for checking dietary needs and allergies before cooking. The tool is explicitly instructed in its system prompt not to suggest recipes containing common allergens (nuts, shellfish, gluten) without flagging them. Final food safety decisions always rest with the volunteer.

**Non-English-speaking volunteers:** The current volunteers both speak English. If a future volunteer has limited English, the tool may produce suggestions they cannot easily read. Mitigation: the kitchen manager will assess language needs before assigning the tool to any new volunteer.

**No protected characteristic is used as input to the system.** The tool receives only ingredient names. It has no knowledge of, and makes no distinction based on, the ethnicity, religion, disability, age, or any other characteristic of any person.
