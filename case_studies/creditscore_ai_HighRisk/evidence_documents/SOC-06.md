# SOC-06: Dual-Use and Misuse Potential Assessed
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Misuse potential assessed: the model could theoretically be repurposed to score applicants outside its validated £1,000–£50,000 personal loan scope (e.g., for mortgages or business lending) with unvalidated results. Technical scope restrictions in GOV-02 and access controls in PRIV-05 prevent this repurposing without a formal, separately-assessed change request.
