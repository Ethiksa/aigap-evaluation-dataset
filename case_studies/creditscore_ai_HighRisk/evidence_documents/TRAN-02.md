# TRAN-02: System Limitations Stated
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Known limitations disclosed to loan officers: known disparity for self-employed applicants pending remediation (FAIR-03/FAIR-04); reduced accuracy for thin-file applicants; no visibility into non-Experian financial history (e.g., overseas credit history). Officers are trained to weigh these limitations when reviewing borderline scores.
