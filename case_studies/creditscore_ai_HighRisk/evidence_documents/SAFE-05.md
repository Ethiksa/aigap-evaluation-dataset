# SAFE-05: Red-Teaming Performed
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

An internal red-team exercise (May 2026) attempted to identify inputs that could game the model into an artificially favourable score (e.g., structuring declared income) and inputs that could produce systematically unfair outcomes for specific groups. Findings fed directly into the FAIR-03 bias testing and SAFE-02 edge-case suite.
