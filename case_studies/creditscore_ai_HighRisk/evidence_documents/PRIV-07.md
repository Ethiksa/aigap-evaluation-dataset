# PRIV-07: Cross-Border Transfer Assessment
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

UK-to-Germany data flows (German resident applications processed via the UK-hosted system) are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses in the intra-group data transfer agreement between FinTrust Bank UK and FinTrust Bank GmbH. A Transfer Impact Assessment (TIA-CREDITSCORE-2026-001) found no significant transfer risk, as Germany remains subject to EU GDPR with equivalent protection.
