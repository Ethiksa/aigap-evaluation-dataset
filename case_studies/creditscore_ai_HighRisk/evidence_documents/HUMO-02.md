# HUMO-02: Human Intervention Points Defined
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Intervention points: (1) every score is reviewed by a loan officer before a decision is communicated; (2) loans above £10,000 require a second officer review; (3) officers can override the score with documented reasoning at any point; (4) the Model Risk Committee can suspend scoring for a segment or entirely, halting all automated scoring pending review.
