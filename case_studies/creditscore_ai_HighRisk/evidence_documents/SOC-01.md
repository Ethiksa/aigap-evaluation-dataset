# SOC-01: Intended Societal Benefit
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

CreditScore AI aims to improve consistency and speed of credit decisions (target: same-day decision vs. 3–5 day baseline) while maintaining fair lending standards. Benefit is measured via quarterly monitoring of decision turnaround time, approval rates by demographic proxy group, and applicant satisfaction, reported to the Board Risk Committee annually.
