# PRIV-08: De-Identification and Anonymization Evaluation
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

No de-identification or anonymisation technique is claimed or relied upon for compliance purposes; all applicant data is treated as identifiable personal data throughout its lifecycle, and all privacy controls (PRIV-01 through PRIV-07) are designed on that basis rather than assuming any anonymisation protection.
