# SEC-06: Pre-Trained Model Supply Chain
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

CreditScore AI does not use a pre-trained foundation model; the XGBoost/LightGBM ensemble is trained from scratch on FinTrust Bank's own historical loan data. Both libraries are open-source with well-established provenance, and their versions are pinned and reviewed under the standard SEC-03 dependency patching process.
