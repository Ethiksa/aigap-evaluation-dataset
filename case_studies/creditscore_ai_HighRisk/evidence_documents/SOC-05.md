# SOC-05: Workforce and Labor Impacts Considered
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

CreditScore AI is designed to assist, not replace, loan officers — it reduces time spent on routine risk assessment, allowing officers to focus on applicant interaction and complex cases. No redundancies are planned as a result of this tool; officer headcount and role scope remain unchanged, confirmed by HR as part of the deployment approval.
