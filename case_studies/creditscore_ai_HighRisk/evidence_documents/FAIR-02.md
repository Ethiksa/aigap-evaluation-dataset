# FAIR-02: Bias Testing Plan
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Methodology: approval-rate disparity analysis across demographic proxies (employment status, postcode area type, credit history length), conducted pre-deployment and monitored monthly thereafter. Acceptable parity threshold: 5% approval-rate disparity between comparable groups. Test datasets: 5 years of historical applications stratified by proxy group.
