# FAIR-03: Bias Testing Executed
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Pre-deployment testing (April 2026) found: self-employed vs. employed applicants with equivalent financial profiles showed an 8.3% approval-rate disparity (above the 5% threshold); thin-file applicants (<24 months credit history) showed 12-point average score underestimation; postcode area type showed no significant disparity after controlling for income and employment status. Full results documented in MFAR-CS-2026-001.
