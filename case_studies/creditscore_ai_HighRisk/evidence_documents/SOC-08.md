# SOC-08: Equity of Access Considered
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

All application channels (digital, branch, phone) provide equal access to CreditScore AI-supported decisions; no channel results in a materially different scoring process. German-language support ensures German applicants are not disadvantaged. Applicants without digital access are not excluded, since branch-based fully human-mediated applications remain available (ACC-06).
