# HUMO-07: Prohibition on Fully Automated Consequential Decisions
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

No credit decision is made by CreditScore AI without loan officer review; this is enforced technically — the system has no capability to communicate a decision directly to an applicant. This satisfies the UK GDPR Article 22 prohibition on decisions "based solely on automated processing" for this rights-affecting use case.
