# SAFE-01: Safety Requirements Defined
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Harm scenarios: incorrect decline harming a creditworthy applicant's access to finance (High severity, mitigated by human review and appeals); incorrect approval leading to unaffordable lending (Medium, mitigated by affordability checks separate from the AI score); systemic disparity harming a demographic group (High, mitigated by FAIR series controls).
