# ACC-07: Harmful UX Patterns Avoided
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

The application form's AI disclosure (TRAN-01) is presented in plain, unbundled language, not buried in a general terms-of-service document. No urgency or pressure language is used around the credit decision. Adverse decision notices clearly and prominently state the applicant's right to request human review, rather than minimising or obscuring it.
