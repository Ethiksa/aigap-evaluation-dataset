# RISK-03: Ethical Impact Assessment
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Ex-ante assessment (April 2026) identified three priority impacts: proxy discrimination against self-employed and thin-file applicants (High priority, see FAIR series), reduced applicant understanding of adverse decisions (Medium, see TRAN-01/TRAN-03), and potential over-reliance by loan officers on the AI score (Medium, see GOV-05). Conducted via structured workshop with Model Risk, Legal, and an external fairness consultant.

**Owner:** Dr. Wei Tan, Head of Model Risk
