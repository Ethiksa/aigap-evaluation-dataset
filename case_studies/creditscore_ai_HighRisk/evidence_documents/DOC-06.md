# DOC-06: Evidence Retention Policy Defined
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Governance evidence (risk assessments, bias test reports, sign-offs) is retained for 7 years, aligned with the FCA record-keeping requirement in PRIV-04/PRIV-05. Access to retained evidence is restricted to Model Risk, Legal, and the DPO, logged via the standard Digital Governance Office access controls.
