# HUMO-03: User Recourse Process Defined
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Applicants receiving an adverse decision are informed of their right to request human review under UK GDPR Article 22, and can request the specific factors that influenced their score via the Customer Relations team. Appeals are handled by a loan officer who did not review the original application, within 10 working days.
