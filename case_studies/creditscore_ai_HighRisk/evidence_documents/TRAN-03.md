# TRAN-03: Explainability Appropriate to Context
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Per-application SHAP value explanations are provided to loan officers, ranking the top 5 factors contributing to each score in plain language (e.g., "credit utilisation above 70% reduced score by 45 points"). A simplified version covering the top 3 factors is generated for applicant-facing adverse decision notices. Recalculated per application, never cached.
