# GOV-02: Documented Purpose and Scope
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

CreditScore AI is a decision-support tool generating a 0–1000 credit risk score for personal loan applications between £1,000–£50,000, for UK and German residents aged 18+. The loan officer retains final decision authority; the score is never the sole basis for a decision. Out of scope: business loans, mortgages, automated rejection without human review, applicants outside the UK/Germany.
