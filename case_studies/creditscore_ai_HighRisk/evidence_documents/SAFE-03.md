# SAFE-03: Robustness to Distribution Shift
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Model performance is monitored monthly against a stability baseline; a Population Stability Index (PSI) exceeding 0.2 on any key input feature triggers a mandatory model review, reflecting the risk that macroeconomic shifts (e.g., interest rate changes) alter the applicant population's characteristics relative to training data.
