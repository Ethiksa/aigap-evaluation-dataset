# ACC-05: Protections for Vulnerable Groups
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

CreditScore AI does not serve minors (applicants must be 18+, verified at application). Vulnerable adult applicants (e.g., those in financial difficulty) receive the same human review and appeal rights as all applicants (HUMO-03); loan officers receive vulnerability-awareness training aligned with FCA Consumer Duty requirements for identifying and supporting vulnerable customers.
