# FAIR-04: Mitigation Actions Documented
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Self-employed disparity: enhanced loan officer guidance and additional income-verification steps introduced, pending a model retrain with expanded self-employment training data (tradeoff: interim reliance on manual officer judgement). Thin-file underestimation: a calibration adjustment factor applied (tradeoff: slightly reduced discrimination between genuinely low- and high-risk thin-file applicants).
