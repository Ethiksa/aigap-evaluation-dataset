# GOV-03: Stakeholder Impact Assessment
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Stakeholders identified: loan applicants (primary risk of incorrect scoring affecting credit access), loan officers (risk of over-reliance on the score), FinTrust Bank (regulatory and reputational risk). Harm pathway: proxy discrimination via employment sector or credit history length disadvantaging self-employed and thin-file applicants, addressed in FAIR-01 through FAIR-04.
