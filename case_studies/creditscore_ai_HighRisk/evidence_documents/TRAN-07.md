# TRAN-07: AI Identity Non-Deception
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

CreditScore AI is an internal staff-facing tool with no direct applicant interaction; applicants never converse with the system. Where the application form or adverse decision notice describes the tool, it is unambiguously described as automated technology, never personified or implied to be a human reviewer.
