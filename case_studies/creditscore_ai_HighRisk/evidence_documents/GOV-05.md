# GOV-05: Human Oversight Plan
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Every credit decision requires loan officer review; the AI score is one input among several. Loans above £10,000 require a second officer review regardless of score. Officers must document reasoning when overriding the AI recommendation. Monthly QA reviews (5% sample) verify oversight is substantive, not rubber-stamped.
