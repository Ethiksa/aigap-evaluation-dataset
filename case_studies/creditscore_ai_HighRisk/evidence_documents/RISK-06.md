# RISK-06: Reassessment Triggers Defined
**CreditScore AI — FinTrust Bank**

Reassessment is triggered by: model retrain or algorithm change, addition of new input features, expansion of served jurisdictions, any fairness metric breach (GOV-07), a confirmed incident (GOV-06), or 12 months elapsed since the last full assessment, whichever occurs first.
