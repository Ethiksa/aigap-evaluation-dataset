# GOV-06: Incident Response and Escalation
## MediScan AI — Incident Response Protocol (IRP-MEDISCAN-2026-001)

**Trigger:** Any false negative, patient harm event, or system failure associated with a MediScan AI output.

**Response steps:**
1. Attending radiologist notifies Clinical Governance Lead within 24 hours.
2. Clinical Governance Lead completes root cause assessment within 72 hours.
3. CMO notified within 48 hours of initial report.
4. If MHRA reportable under UK MDR 2002 Article 87 — MHRA notified within 15 days.
5. All patients in the same scan batch reviewed within 7 days.
6. Head of AI Engineering suspends affected model version pending investigation.

**Post-incident:** Full review completed within 30 days. Findings reported to Clinical AI Review Board.

**Document Owner:** Chief Medical Officer | June 2026
