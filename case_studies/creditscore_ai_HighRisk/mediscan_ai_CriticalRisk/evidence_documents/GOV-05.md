# GOV-05: Human Oversight Plan
## MediScan AI — Radiologist Review Protocol

**Policy:** All MediScan AI diagnostic outputs are advisory only. No clinical action may be taken without consultant radiologist sign-off.

**Review requirements:**
- Every AI report must be reviewed and signed off by a GMC-registered consultant radiologist before entering the patient record.
- High-confidence cancer flags (score >0.85) trigger on-call radiologist notification within 2 hours.
- Disputed findings are escalated to a second consultant within 48 hours.

**Escalation path:** Radiographer → On-call Radiologist → Clinical Governance Lead → CMO

**Audit:** Clinical Governance Lead reviews weekly AI output logs for any deviation from mandatory review process.

**Document Owner:** Chief Medical Officer | June 2026
