# SOC-01: Societal Benefit Statement
## MediScan AI — Clinical Impact and Equity Plan

**Intended benefit:** Improve early detection of Stage I and II lung cancer, pneumonia, and tuberculosis in hospital settings across the UK and Sri Lanka.

**Target outcome:** 15% improvement in early-stage lung cancer detection rates compared to radiologist-only diagnosis, measured against the pre-deployment clinical audit baseline.

**Equity monitoring:**
- Diagnostic accuracy across UK and Sri Lanka patient populations reviewed quarterly.
- Any disparity exceeding 5 percentage points between populations triggers a model review.
- Paediatric accuracy monitored separately given age-specific validation constraints.

**Societal risk:** Overconfidence in AI outputs by radiologists. Mitigated by mandatory human review, advisory-only labelling, and training that emphasises clinical judgment primacy.

**Benefit measurement:** Annual clinical outcomes review comparing AI-assisted versus pre-AI detection rates. Results reported to hospital boards and Clinical AI Review Board.
