# GOV-04: Risk Assessment

**Design-time assessment:** A risk assessment was completed in May 2026 by the LSO team with input from the university's Digital Governance Office. The assessment classified StudySpark AI as low risk given its assistive-only role, restricted staff-only access, and mandatory human review before any output reaches a student.

**Assumptions used:** Staff will always review and personalise drafts before sending; the tool will not be given access to send messages directly; usage remains within the LSO team.

**Reassessment plan:** The risk assessment will be repeated before any decision to expand access beyond the LSO or to allow direct messaging capability, and otherwise reviewed annually.
