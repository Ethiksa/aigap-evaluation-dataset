# RISK-01: System Context

**Domain:** Internal higher-education administrative support tool.

**Stakeholders:** Seven LSO staff (direct users); students engaging with the LSO (indirectly affected via the messages staff send).

**Scale:** 7 users; approximately 40–60 drafting sessions per week during term time.

**Decision impact:** None — the tool drafts text only. No decision about a student's academic standing, support eligibility, or extensions is made or influenced by the tool itself.

**Operating environment:** University-managed devices, internal SSO-gated portal, Azure OpenAI EU-region processing.

**Dependencies:** Azure OpenAI GPT-4o API; university SSO authentication.
