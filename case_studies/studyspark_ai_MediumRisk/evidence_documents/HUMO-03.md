# HUMO-03: User Recourse Process

**Who might want recourse:** A student who feels a guidance message they received was unhelpful or inaccurate.

**Recourse path:** Students can respond to any LSO message and request a follow-up conversation with staff, exactly as they could before StudySpark AI existed. LSO staff remain the accountable point of contact for all guidance provided, regardless of whether a message was AI-assisted or written from scratch.

Because every message is staff-reviewed and staff-attributed, students are never interacting with an unmediated AI output, so no AI-specific complaint mechanism is required beyond the existing LSO contact process.
