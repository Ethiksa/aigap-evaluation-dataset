# FAIR-07: User Recourse and Appeals

**Context:** Because every StudySpark AI draft is reviewed, edited, and personally sent by an LSO staff member, no fairness-related harm can occur without a human's direct involvement and accountability.

**Recourse route:** If a student believes the guidance they received was unhelpful or inappropriate, they can request a follow-up conversation with the same or a different LSO staff member through the normal LSO contact channel — exactly as they could before this tool existed.

No AI-specific appeals mechanism is required beyond the existing LSO complaint and follow-up process, since the AI never interacts with a student directly or unmediated.
