# GOV-03: Stakeholder Impact Assessment

**Who could be affected:**
- Students receiving guidance messages — could be helped by clearer, more consistent advice, or mildly harmed by a poorly drafted message if staff do not review carefully.
- LSO staff — benefit from reduced drafting time; risk of over-reliance on AI drafts without careful review.

**Pathways of harm:** The main pathway is staff sending a drafted message without adequate review, potentially containing inaccurate or generic advice. This is mitigated by the mandatory human review step (see GOV-05, HUMO-02) — no message can be sent without staff editing and approval.

No pathway exists for the tool to directly affect academic decisions, funding, or student status.
