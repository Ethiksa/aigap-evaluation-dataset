# SOC-08: Equity of Access Considered

**Context:** StudySpark AI is a staff-facing internal tool. Students do not access it directly, so student-facing equity concerns around cost, language, or device access do not apply to the tool itself.

**Staff equity:** All seven LSO staff have equal access via the same SSO-gated portal on university-provided devices. No staff member faces a cost, language, or infrastructure barrier to using the tool.

**Indirect equity consideration:** Because the tool may help staff respond to students more quickly and consistently, it could modestly improve equity of support quality across students, provided all staff apply the same review standard — this is monitored through Dr. Marsh's monthly sample review.

**Owner:** Dr. Fiona Marsh, Head of Learning Support | June 2026
