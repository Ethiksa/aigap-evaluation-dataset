# GOV-06: Incident Response and Escalation

**Trigger:** Any harmful, inaccurate, or inappropriate output from StudySpark AI reported by a staff member.

**Response steps:**
1. Staff member reports the issue to Dr. Fiona Marsh within 24 hours.
2. Dr. Marsh reviews the specific interaction and determines whether it reflects a one-off issue or a pattern requiring a system prompt change.
3. If a student was sent an affected message before review caught the issue, the LSO team follows up with the student directly.
4. Any pattern of concerning outputs pauses use of the tool pending review by the Digital Governance Office.

**Owner:** Dr. Fiona Marsh, Head of Learning Support | June 2026
