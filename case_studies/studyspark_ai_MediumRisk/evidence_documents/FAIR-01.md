# FAIR-01: Protected Groups Considered

**Groups considered:**
- Students with disabilities or additional learning needs — staff are instructed not to enter disability-related details into the tool; such cases are routed through the existing Disability Support process instead, so the AI tool has no role in disability-related guidance.
- International and non-native English-speaking students — the system prompt instructs the model to use plain, clear language, and staff are trained to adjust tone and complexity when personalising drafts for these students.
- First-generation university students — considered during the ethical impact assessment (RISK-03); no differential treatment identified since the tool only assists with drafting, not decision-making.

No protected characteristic is used as an input to the system.
