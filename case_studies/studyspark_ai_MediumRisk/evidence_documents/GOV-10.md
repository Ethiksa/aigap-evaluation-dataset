# GOV-10: Regulatory Compliance Mapping

**Applicable obligations:**
- UK GDPR — applies because student difficulty summaries and course details are personal data.
- Ashcombe University Data Protection Policy — internal policy governing staff use of third-party AI tools with student-related information.
- Azure OpenAI Data Processing Agreement — governs the university's contractual relationship with Microsoft for EU-hosted processing.

**Design mapping:** UK GDPR is addressed through PRIV-01 through PRIV-06 controls (minimisation, lawful basis, notice, retention, access control, provenance). The university's Digital Governance Office reviewed and approved the tool's compliance mapping in May 2026 prior to pilot launch.

No EU AI Act obligations apply as the university operates UK-only with no EU market placement.
