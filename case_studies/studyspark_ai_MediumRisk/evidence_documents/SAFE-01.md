# SAFE-01: Safety Requirements and Harm Scenarios

**Identified harm scenarios:**

| Scenario | Severity | Safeguard |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| Staff sends draft without personalising | Medium | Mandatory review step; staff briefing; monthly sampling by Dr. Marsh |
| Tool suggests advice inconsistent with current policy | Low | Staff trained to verify policy-specific details independently |
| Disability or health data entered by mistake | Medium | System prompt does not request this data; staff trained not to enter it |
| Tool unavailable during high-demand period | Minimal | Staff draft messages manually, as they did before the tool existed |

**No safety-critical scenarios identified.** The tool has no ability to directly contact students, make decisions, or take autonomous action.
