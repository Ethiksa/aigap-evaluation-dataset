# RISK-02: Risk Classification

**Classification: Limited risk.**

**Rationale:** StudySpark AI processes personal data (course, year, brief difficulty description) and is used in an educational support context, which places it above minimal risk. However, it does not make or influence any decision affecting a student's rights, academic standing, or access to services — every output is staff-reviewed and staff-attributed before reaching a student. Access is restricted to seven trained staff. This combination of moderate data sensitivity with zero decision-making authority supports a limited-risk classification, consistent with the AIGAP PG (Postgraduate) tier and a raw score of 52/100.

No mandatory overrides apply — no children's data, no rights-affecting decisions, no regulated sector deployment, no safety-critical function.
