# PRIV-02: Lawful Basis and Consent

**Lawful basis:** UK GDPR Article 6(1)(e) — processing necessary for the performance of a task carried out in the public interest, specifically the university's academic support function for enrolled students.

**Consent context:** Students engage with the LSO voluntarily when reporting academic difficulties. The LSO privacy notice, provided to all students at enrolment, covers the use of internal digital tools including AI-assisted drafting to support staff communications.

No special category data is processed, so no Article 9 basis is required. Where a student's difficulty relates to a disability, staff are instructed not to enter this detail into StudySpark AI and to handle such cases through the existing Disability Support process instead.
