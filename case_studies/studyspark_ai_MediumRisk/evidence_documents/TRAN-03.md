# TRAN-03: Explainability Appropriate to Context

**Explanation for staff:** Each draft includes a one-line note on which details from the input were used, for example: "Draft focuses on time management strategies based on the described coursework deadline struggle."

**Why this is appropriate:** Staff are professional advisers who exercise their own judgement over every draft before sending. A detailed technical explanation of the model's internal reasoning would add no practical value to their review process. The one-line rationale is sufficient to help staff quickly assess whether the draft has correctly understood the situation described.

No student-facing explanation is required since students never see AI output directly — they see only the staff-reviewed final message.
