# TRAN-01: User-Facing AI Disclosure

**Staff disclosure:** The StudySpark AI interface is clearly labelled "AI Drafting Assistant" and every session begins with the message: "This tool generates a draft using AI. You must review and personalise it before sending."

**Student disclosure:** The LSO privacy notice given to all students explains that staff may use AI-assisted drafting tools, and every message sent to a student is signed by the named staff member who reviewed and sent it — never presented as coming from an AI system.
