# TRAN-04: Model Card — StudySpark AI
## Ashcombe University Learning Support Office | June 2026

**System name:** StudySpark AI
**Model used:** Azure OpenAI GPT-4o (EU region deployment)
**Intended users:** 7 LSO staff members

### Intended Use
Drafting suggested guidance messages for LSO staff to review, personalise, and send to students who have reported academic difficulties.

### Out-of-Scope Use
Direct student communication, any decision-making about academic standing or support eligibility, use outside the LSO team, entry of disability or health-related data.

### Data
No Ashcombe-specific training data used. No fine-tuning performed. Session inputs are course, year, and a brief difficulty description only.

### Known Limitations
No memory between sessions; no access to the student information system; may occasionally suggest advice inconsistent with recently changed policy; English only.

**Owner:** Dr. Fiona Marsh, Head of Learning Support | June 2026
