# ACC-07: Harmful UX Patterns Avoided

**Design review:** The StudySpark AI interface was reviewed against the university's UX design guidelines in May 2026 to confirm it avoids dark patterns.

**Specific measures:**
- No default "send" action exists — every message requires an explicit staff review and send step, with the draft clearly editable before sending.
- No urgency or pressure language is used to encourage staff to skip review.
- The AI-generated nature of the draft is never obscured or minimised in the interface (see TRAN-01).
- No consent bundling or confusing disclosure exists — the single privacy notice covers AI tool use in plain language.
