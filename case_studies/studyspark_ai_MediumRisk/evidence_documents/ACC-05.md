# ACC-05: Protections for Vulnerable Groups

**Applicability:** StudySpark AI's direct user population is LSO staff, not students, and the declared user population does not include minors (all Ashcombe students are 18+) or a marginalized group as a primary user category.

**Indirect protection for vulnerable students:** Where a student's underlying difficulty relates to a disability or mental health concern, staff are explicitly trained and instructed by system design not to enter this detail into StudySpark AI. These cases are handled entirely through the university's existing Disability Support and Wellbeing processes, which have their own established safeguards and are outside the scope of this tool.

This separation ensures StudySpark AI plays no role in guidance for the university's most vulnerable student cases.
