# HUMO-01: Oversight Roles Defined and Trained

**Oversight role:** All seven LSO staff members act as the reviewing party for every StudySpark AI draft they generate. No separate reviewer role exists — the requesting staff member is also the reviewer, consistent with how they already personally review any communication they send to students.

**Training:** All LSO staff completed a 30-minute briefing in May 2026 covering how to use the tool, the requirement to always personalise drafts, and how to report concerning outputs. Training completion is logged in the LSO staff training register.
