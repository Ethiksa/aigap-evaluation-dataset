# FAIR-02: Bias Testing Plan

**Context:** StudySpark AI drafts suggested text only; it does not score, rank, or make any decision about students, so formal statistical bias testing methodologies designed for decision-making systems are not directly applicable.

**Proportionate approach adopted:** During the first pilot term, Dr. Marsh reviews a monthly sample of 15 drafts to check for tone or content differences correlated with the type of difficulty described (e.g. whether messages about mental health concerns are handled with appropriately different care and sensitivity than messages about time management).

**Threshold:** If any staff member or the monthly review identifies a pattern of inappropriate or inconsistent tone across similar cases, the system prompt will be revised and Dr. Marsh will report the finding to the Digital Governance Office.
