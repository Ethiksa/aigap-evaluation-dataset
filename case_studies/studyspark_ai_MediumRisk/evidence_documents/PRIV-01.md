# PRIV-01: Data Minimisation

**Data sent to the model:** Only the student's course, year of study, and a brief free-text description of the reported difficulty (for example, "struggling with time management for coursework deadlines"). No student name, ID number, or contact details are included in the prompt.

**Data excluded by design:** Student name, student ID, email address, any health or disability information, any information about other students, and any information not directly relevant to the guidance being drafted.

Staff are trained to refer to students by role only (e.g. "a second-year Biology student") when using the tool, never by name.
