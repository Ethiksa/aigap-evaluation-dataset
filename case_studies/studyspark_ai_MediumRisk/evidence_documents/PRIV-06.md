# PRIV-06: Data Provenance and Rights

**Provenance:** All data entered into StudySpark AI originates directly from LSO staff during their own case notes process — no external or purchased dataset is used, and no training data was collected from students for this tool.

**Rights:** No third-party data rights apply since the system uses a general-purpose pre-trained model (GPT-4o) rather than any Ashcombe-specific training data. Student information entered as prompts is Ashcombe University data governed by the standard staff data handling agreement, not shared with or licensed to any third party beyond the Azure OpenAI processing itself.

**Owner:** Data Protection Officer, Ashcombe University | June 2026
