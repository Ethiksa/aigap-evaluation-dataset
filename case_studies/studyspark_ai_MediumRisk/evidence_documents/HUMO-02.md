# HUMO-02: Human Intervention Points

**Intervention point:** The sole and mandatory intervention point is before sending — every draft is read, edited, and manually sent by the requesting staff member. There is no automated pathway from draft generation to student delivery.

**Override:** Staff can discard any draft entirely and write their own message from scratch, which happens in practice for more sensitive cases.

**Halt:** If Dr. Marsh identifies a systemic issue, she can disable staff access to the tool immediately by removing it from the SSO portal.
