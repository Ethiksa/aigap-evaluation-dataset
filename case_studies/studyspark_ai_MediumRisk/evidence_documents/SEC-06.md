# SEC-06: Pre-Trained Model Supply Chain

**Base model:** Azure OpenAI GPT-4o, accessed via Microsoft's Azure OpenAI Service (EU region).

**Provenance:** Microsoft's Azure OpenAI Service is a Microsoft-managed deployment of OpenAI's GPT-4o model, governed by Microsoft's enterprise data processing terms and the university's existing Azure enterprise agreement.

**Licensing:** Use is governed by the university's Azure OpenAI Service agreement, reviewed and approved by university procurement and the Digital Governance Office in April 2026.

**Known limitations and safety testing:** Microsoft publishes model transparency documentation for Azure OpenAI models, reviewed by the Digital Governance Office prior to approval. Microsoft's built-in content filtering is enabled on the deployment.

**Alignment with intended use:** GPT-4o's general text drafting capability is well-suited to the assistive drafting task; no fine-tuning was required or performed.

**Owner:** Digital Governance Office, Ashcombe University | June 2026
