# GOV-02: Documented Purpose and Scope

**Purpose:** StudySpark AI drafts personalised study guidance message suggestions for LSO staff to review, edit, and send to students who have reported academic difficulties.

**Intended users:** The seven LSO staff members only.

**Explicit out-of-scope uses:** Sending any message without staff review; making or implying any decision about extensions, mitigating circumstances, or academic standing; use by any staff outside the LSO; use with any student not already engaged with the LSO.

The tool drafts suggestions only — it never contacts a student directly.

**Owner:** Dr. Fiona Marsh, Head of Learning Support | June 2026
