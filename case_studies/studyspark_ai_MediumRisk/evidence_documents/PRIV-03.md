# PRIV-03: Notice and Transparency

**How students are informed:** The LSO privacy notice, given to every student at their first LSO appointment, includes a paragraph stating that LSO staff may use AI-assisted drafting tools to help prepare guidance communications, and that all such communications are reviewed and personalised by a staff member before being sent.

**How staff are informed:** The onboarding briefing (see HUMO-01) explains what data is sent to Azure OpenAI and why, and staff sign an acknowledgement confirming they understand the data minimisation requirements.
