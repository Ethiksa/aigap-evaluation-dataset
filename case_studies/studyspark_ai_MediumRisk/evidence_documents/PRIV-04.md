# PRIV-04: Data Retention and Deletion

**Retention:** StudySpark AI does not store any query or draft after the staff member closes the session. The system is stateless by design.

**Azure OpenAI retention:** The university's Azure OpenAI EU deployment is configured with the abuse monitoring opt-out and zero additional data retention beyond the transient processing required to generate a response.

**Sent messages:** The final, staff-edited message sent to a student is retained as part of the student's standard LSO case record under the university's existing records retention schedule (6 years post-graduation), unrelated to StudySpark AI itself.
