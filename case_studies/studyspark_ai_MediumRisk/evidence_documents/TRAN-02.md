# TRAN-02: System Limitations Stated

**Limitations communicated to staff (displayed in the tool interface):**
- "I don't know this student's full history — always check their case notes before sending."
- "I may suggest generic advice — please personalise before sending."
- "I don't have access to disability or health information — do not enter this data."

**Additional limitations from staff briefing:** The tool has no memory between sessions, cannot access the student information system, and may occasionally suggest advice inconsistent with the university's current academic policies if those policies have recently changed — staff are reminded to verify policy-specific details independently.
