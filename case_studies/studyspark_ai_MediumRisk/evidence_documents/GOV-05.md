# GOV-05: Human Oversight Plan

**Oversight model:** Every StudySpark AI draft is reviewed by the LSO staff member who requested it before any message is sent to a student. No automated sending capability exists — the tool has no email or messaging integration.

**Review point:** Staff read the full draft, edit as needed to reflect the specific student's circumstances, and send it manually through the university's standard student communication system.

**Intervention:** If a draft seems inappropriate or inaccurate, the staff member simply does not use it and drafts the message themselves. Dr. Marsh reviews a 10% sample of sent messages monthly to confirm the review process is working as intended.

**Owner:** Dr. Fiona Marsh, Head of Learning Support | June 2026
