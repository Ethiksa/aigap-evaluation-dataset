# RISK-03: Ethical Impact Assessment

**Assessment completed:** May 2026, prior to pilot launch, by the LSO team with the Digital Governance Office.

**Impacts identified and prioritised:**
1. Risk of generic, impersonal guidance if staff over-rely on drafts without personalising — Medium priority, mitigated by mandatory review requirement.
2. Risk of staff time savings reducing genuine one-to-one engagement quality — Low priority, monitored via staff feedback sessions.
3. Risk of data minimisation lapses if staff enter identifying details — Medium priority, mitigated by training and system prompt design that does not request identifying fields.

**Identification method:** Structured workshop with LSO staff and a Digital Governance Office ethics reviewer, using the university's standard AI ethical impact checklist.
