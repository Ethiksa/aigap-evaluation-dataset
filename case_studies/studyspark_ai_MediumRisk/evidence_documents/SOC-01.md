# SOC-01: Intended Societal Benefit

**Intended benefit:** StudySpark AI helps LSO staff spend less time on routine drafting and more time on substantive student engagement, potentially improving the consistency and speed of academic support guidance for students who are struggling.

**Who benefits:** LSO staff gain drafting time savings; students benefit from potentially faster and more consistent guidance responses.

**How benefit is recognised:** Dr. Marsh tracks average time-to-response for LSO guidance messages before and during the pilot, and gathers informal student feedback through existing LSO satisfaction surveys.

**Societal risk:** Minimal — the tool operates entirely within an existing, well-established student support process and does not create new dependencies or displace human judgement.

**Owner:** Dr. Fiona Marsh, Head of Learning Support | June 2026
