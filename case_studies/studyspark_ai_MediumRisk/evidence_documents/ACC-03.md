# ACC-03: Human Factors Hazards Assessed

**Automation bias risk:** Staff might come to trust AI drafts without adequate scrutiny over time, especially during busy periods. Mitigated through initial training emphasising the mandatory personalisation requirement, and Dr. Marsh's monthly sample review specifically checking for signs of insufficiently edited drafts.

**Over-trust risk:** Staff might assume the tool has access to information it does not have (e.g. the student's full case history). Mitigated by the tool's limitations being clearly stated in-interface (TRAN-02) and covered in staff training.

**Workflow harm risk:** If staff become dependent on the tool and it becomes unavailable, workflow could be disrupted. Mitigated by the fallback behaviour (SAFE-04) which allows staff to fall back to manual drafting, which is how they operated before the tool existed.
